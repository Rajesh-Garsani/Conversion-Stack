# Design Research Notes

The visual system and conversion flow were informed by publicly visible patterns from ten established U.S. digital product and software agencies. No source code, brand assets, proprietary graphics, or text was copied.

## Agencies reviewed

1. [Work & Co](https://work.co/) — direct problem-led positioning, selective work, strong inquiry path
2. [Huge](https://www.hugeinc.com/solutions) — outcome framing, clear solution categories, measurable business language
3. [Instrument](https://www.instrument.com/) — expressive typography, integrated brand/product/technology presentation
4. [thoughtbot](https://thoughtbot.com/) — high-trust engineering language, services mapped to company stages
5. [Clay](https://clay.global/) — polished case-study presentation, generous scale, concise capability hierarchy
6. [Viget](https://www.viget.com/) — practical service navigation, direct calls to action, visible process expertise
7. [Fueled](https://fueled.com/) — outcome-led case-study headlines, strong expertise grouping, human tone
8. [DockYard](https://dockyard.com/) — concise custom-product positioning and early consultation call to action
9. [Utility](https://utility.agency/) — focused product-development positioning and straightforward contact routing
10. [WillowTree / TELUS Digital](https://www.telusdigital.com/willowtree-is-now-telus-digital) — enterprise-scale credibility and transformation framing

## Patterns applied in the original design

- A short, problem-led hero with two clear next actions
- Selected work shown before long company explanations
- Services described through business outcomes and concrete capabilities
- High-contrast typography, generous spacing, restrained motion, and strong visual rhythm
- Trust sections that remain honest when real client logos or reviews are not yet available
- Repeated contextual calls to action instead of one isolated contact button
- Case studies structured as problem, response, capabilities, media, and impact
- A detailed contact form that accepts an imperfect brief and explains the next step
- Mobile-first navigation, reduced-motion support, accessible focus treatment, and useful empty/error states
